Seam studio

Drag the seam between map and photograph. Copy the path into the zone's manifest entry.

Drag any point. Click the dashed seam to add one — or Double-click anywhere, or use Add point. Alt-click a point to remove it. The two end points stay pinned to their edges so the shape always closes. Feather previews MASK_FEATHER_PX and is not part of the path. Test on the slides opens the real piece using this seam.

Paste into the zone in assets/zones.js as mask: '…' — with photoSide: 'right' beside it if the side differs from the default. Or replace the matching entry in MASK_SHAPES in assets/scroll.js to change every zone at once.
