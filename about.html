Scope

მოცულობა

The project holds boundaries for five regions so far — Kakheti, Kartli, Imereti, Racha-Lechkhumi and Samegrelo — and covers them one page at a time. The hub draws all of them wherever they fall, whether or not the region has a page yet, so the map is never further ahead of the writing than the data is.

The boundaries

საზღვრები

Provenance: who delineated these microzones, under what instrument or regulation, at what scale, and in which year. This is the paragraph a reader will judge the whole project by — it should say plainly how the lines were arrived at and what their positional accuracy is.

Nine boundary files are in hand. Each is a single-part polygon in WGS 84, and their sizes span more than an order of magnitude — from a zone you can walk across in half an hour to one that takes in a whole valley floor.

Extents are bounding-box dimensions, not areas, measured at each zone's own latitude. Vertex counts are as supplied; the piece thins them to a 6 m tolerance before drawing, which removes roughly 87 % of them without a visible change to the shape.

The imagery

სატელიტური სურათები

Mapbox Satellite, a mosaic of Maxar, Landsat and Sentinel-2 sources whose capture dates vary by tile — so the vineyard patterns visible inside one zone were not necessarily photographed in the same season as those in the next. Anything read off the imagery about plantings should be treated as indicative, not as a survey.

The topographic sheets

ტოპოგრაფიული რუკები

Soviet military topographic mapping at 1:50 000, surveyed 1973–1990, requested from the WMS service run by the National Agency of Public Registry (NAPR) and published through the NSDI geoportal. Requested as JPEG rather than PNG — 300 KB against 1.1 MB for the same frame — at the cost of an alpha channel, which is not needed here because the patch mask supplies transparency.

The sheets are not a map layer. They are composited into each zone's colour patch, in the same canvas as the imagery, so they appear only inside the boundary and fade out with it — everything outside stays plain black-and-white imagery. The blend is multiply at 0.85, which keeps satellite texture visible through the sheet's paper white instead of covering it.

One request per zone, for exactly the patch extent in EPSG:3857 at the patch's pixel size — about 2200 × 2560, which the service renders in a little over a second. Requesting it in the projection and at the size the canvas is already in means no resampling of our own between sheet and imagery. Survey dates are per sheet and not recorded here; treat the vintage as approximate.

Parcels

ნაკვეთები

A zone may carry a parcel layer: individual vineyard boundaries with the varieties planted on them, drawn only when a reader asks for it. Nothing is stored here — the parcels are fetched live from the National Agency of Public Registry's vineyard cadastre for the zone's own extent, at the moment the button is pressed.

Parcels are fetched for a little more than the zone — up to 1.6 km past its edge — and the neighbours are drawn dimmed, so a zone is not a lone island of vineyards in an empty frame. They are excluded from every number. That distinction is not cosmetic: the service answers with everything intersecting a rectangle, and for ახოები the rectangle holds 752 parcels while the boundary holds 528. Counting the rectangle would overstate the zone by 46 %. Each parcel is tested against the boundary itself before it reaches the totals.

Three things about that service shape what you see. Its query endpoint returns no geometry, so identify is the geometry endpoint. It caps a response at 2,000 rows and ignores paging, so a dense extent is swept by splitting it into quadrants until every cell comes back under the cap — ახოები takes one request and about two seconds, a 14 km view takes seventeen and about forty. And a row is not a parcel: each carries one grape variety, so rows are dissolved to one feature per polygon with the varieties collected onto it. Where a sweep still hits its limit the panel says so rather than presenting a partial count as a total.

Colour carries the vine: white grapes gold, coloured grapes claret, and a neutral for parcels the register has not yet surveyed. That is viticulture's own primary split rather than an arbitrary index, and it is the one the data encodes — a berry colour is derived from each variety name.

Two classes and not five, for a reason worth stating. A parcel map is a choropleth, so any two colours can end up adjacent and every pair has to separate. Holding white, black, red and pink apart fails that test: gold against red separates by only 4.8–5.6 for a deuteranope and rosé against claret by 11.2 even with full colour vision, against floors of 8 and 15. Red and pink sit too close to both gold and claret once every pair must work. Folding them in with black is not a concession — white against coloured is the split winemaking itself makes, and in ახოები red is three rows out of 856. The pair in use clears every check with room to spare: colour-blind separation 19.9 against a floor of 8, normal vision 23.2 against 15. Each class also names its varieties in the table, so identity never rests on colour alone. A parcel map is a choropleth, so any two colours can end up adjacent, and on that all-pairs test a fourth hue collapses: yellow against orange separates by ΔE 4.8 for a deuteranope and 10.6 even with full colour vision, against floors of 8 and 15. The three in use were validated against the dark surface at worst-pair ΔE 9.4. Every colour is also named in the accompanying table, so identity never rests on colour alone.

Within each family the vines are stepped by shade: the darkest is what the family is mostly planted to, the next its runner-up, the lightest everything else. Two things are shown at once and they are not the same kind of thing — family is an identity, rank within a family is an order — so each takes the channel that suits it, hue for one and lightness for the other. Each ramp validates on its own terms against the dark surface (monotone lightness, adjacent gaps over 0.06, a single hue at 5° and 4° of spread), and the families never collide: across every gold-against-claret pair the worst separation is ΔE 18.9 for a deuteranope and 21.2 to full colour vision. So a reader who cannot tell two golds apart can still always tell gold from claret, which is the distinction that carries the meaning. Three steps and no more — past that the steps stop being separable and the ramp starts implying a precision it does not have.

Two tabs colour the same parcels two ways. Vine is the split above. Planted takes the year, which is a magnitude rather than an identity, so it gets a sequential ramp instead: one hue, light to dark, oldest darkest — never a spread of hues, which would imply categories where there is an order. Blue keeps it clearly apart from the vine tab. The five breaks are cut to the data rather than to round decades: Kakheti's plantings are bimodal, a Soviet peak in the 1980s and a replanting boom in the 2010s with the 1990s thin between them, and these breaks put each peak in its own class instead of splitting one across two. The ramp is validated as ordinal against the dark surface — monotone lightness, every adjacent gap over 0.06, 3° of hue spread, darkest step clearing the surface at 2.15:1.

A zone's colour arrives in two passes. The satellite frame paints first; the topographic sheet is blended in when it lands, which on that host can be several seconds later. It is capped at 1200 px — a 1:50 000 sheet scanned at 300 dpi holds about 4 m per pixel, so asking for more only makes the server upsample a scan.

Hovering a parcel gives its varieties with the area, planting year and training of each. Inside the boundary the fills are solid with a white hairline between neighbours; outside they stay a faint wash, so context never competes with the subject.

Relief

რელიეფი

Depth comes from two passes at different scales. The Studio style's own shading tileset — the one the inset locator uses — reads the region: where the Caucasus front gives way to the Alazani valley floor. Its tiles stop at zoom 11 and it is light-on-white shading built for a pale basemap, so it is faded out as the camera closes in rather than shown overzoomed and fogging the imagery.

Closer in, a hillshade computed from Mapbox Terrain-DEM takes over, with its exaggeration ramped from 0.7 at regional zoom to 1.15 over a zone. It is drawn above both the black-and-white surround and the zone's colour patch, so one relief pass lights the whole frame consistently — which is what lets a slope read as a slope and a terrace as a terrace.

What the map does not do

რას არ აკეთებს რუკა

  • Field patterns visible inside a zone are imagery, not data. Where a zone has a parcel layer, the parcels are data and the imagery around them is not — the two should not be read as one.
  • It does not distinguish planted from unplanted land inside a boundary.
  • It carries no elevation or aspect analysis, though the terrain is shaded.
  • It does not georectify anything itself. The topographic sheets are placed by the WMS service; any misregistration between a sheet and the imagery is upstream, and at 1:50 000 a plotting error of half a millimetre on paper is 25 m on the ground.

The look

ვიზუალური ენა

The site is set as a page from an ampelography — the illustrated grape monographs of the nineteenth century — because that is the tradition this project sits in: naming vines and drawing where they grow. Warm laid paper, iron-gall ink, small caps for captions, and the plates' own inks for accent: the green of a leaf, the gold of the fruit, the russet of a cut cane. The grain is drawn rather than photographed — a turbulence filter at 5 % opacity over a warm wash — so it costs nothing to download and tiles forever.

The change is not only cosmetic. Every colour on the map had been stepped for a near-black surface; on paper the lightest gold read at 1.28:1, which is no mark at all. All three ramps were re-stepped and re-validated against the paper the legend is printed on.

Colophon

კოლოფონი

Built as a static site — no build step, three stylesheets and two scripts. Mapbox GL JS 3.9 for the map, Scrollama for the scroll steps, EB Garamond for text and Fraunces for display. The zone manifest lives in assets/zones.js; the machinery is in assets/scroll.js and is not meant to be edited to add a zone.
