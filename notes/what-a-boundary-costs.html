What a boundary costs

რა ღირს საზღვარი

ამ გვერდის ძირითადი ტექსტი ჯერ ინგლისურადაა. სათაურები და რუკის ინტერფეისი ქართულადაა; სტატიის თარგმანი მოგვიანებით დაემატება.

The nine files carry 16,426 vertices between them. Two of them account for more than half.

24 August 2026 · build note

Boundary files arrive at whatever precision the tool that made them happened to emit. In this set the range is wide: ყვარელი-ქინძმარაული has 7,649 vertices, მაღრაანის ქისი has 114 — and both describe a shape that will be drawn at most a few hundred pixels across.

Vertex count matters here more than it usually does, because the geometry isn't drawn once. Every zone's colour patch is masked by walking its boundary path into a canvas, and the piece re-runs that as you scroll. Simplification is not an optimisation to add later; it's part of getting the interaction to feel like nothing at all.

Douglas-Peucker, in metres

The tolerance wants to be in ground units, not degrees — a degree of longitude at this latitude is about a quarter shorter than a degree of latitude, so a tolerance expressed in degrees thins the shape unevenly along its two axes. Projecting each ring to metres first, then running Douglas-Peucker at a 6 m tolerance, gives:

Nearly nine tenths of the geometry goes, and nothing visible changes. Six metres is under two pixels at the zoom these zones are framed at, so the displacement is smaller than the line that would draw it.

The interesting row is the last one. მაღრაანის ქისი loses only a third of its vertices, because it was digitised sparsely to begin with — its points are already further apart than the tolerance. A file that resists simplification is telling you it was drawn coarsely, which is worth knowing before you quote a boundary to the metre.

Simplify once, for everything

The thinning runs on load, on the source geometry, before anything is drawn — not per-consumer. That matters because three separate things draw these shapes: the colour patch mask, the inset locator, and the story panels' framing. Simplify only the mask and the shapes drift apart by a pixel or two, which shows up as the inset's outline sitting slightly off its own fill. One tolerance, applied once, keeps them identical.

SIMPLIFY_M in assets/scroll.js sets the tolerance; 0 disables thinning entirely if you ever need the supplied geometry drawn verbatim.
